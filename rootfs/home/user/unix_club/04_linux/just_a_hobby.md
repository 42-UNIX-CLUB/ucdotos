# Linux: "just a hobby"

  comp.os.minix . 25 Aug 1991
  From: torvalds@klaava.Helsinki.FI (Linus Benedict Torvalds)

    Hello everybody out there using minix -
    I'm doing a (free) operating system (just a hobby, won't be big
    and professional like gnu) for 386(486) AT clones.

  * 1991  Linus Torvalds, a 21-year-old CS student in Helsinki, starts
          writing a kernel
  * 1992  relicensed under the GPL -> GNU + Linux = a complete free OS.
          Tanenbaum (MINIX's author): "LINUX is obsolete"
  * 1996  Tux the penguin becomes the mascot
  * 2005  Linus writes git. yes, that git.
  * today every TOP500 supercomputer. so much for "won't be big and
          professional"

  "Talk is cheap. Show me the code."  Linus Torvalds, 2000
