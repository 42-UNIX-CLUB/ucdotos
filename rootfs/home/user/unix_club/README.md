A student club at 42 Lisboa for the UNIX philosophy, customization &
ricing, on Linux, the BSDs & macOS. Founded by rucosta & mju-ferr.

  01 educational
     sharing & demystifying the UNIX philosophy, Linux fundamentals and
     comfort in the terminal

  02 execution & showcasing
     hands-on ricing, customization & dotfiles, and showing off members'
     own setups

  03 peer support
     a place to learn how the UNIX & Linux bits of your projects work
     (Born2beroot & beyond). A resource to learn from, not a help desk.
