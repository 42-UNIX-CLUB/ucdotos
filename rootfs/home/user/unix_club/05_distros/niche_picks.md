# Niche picks

  $ ls ~/distro-hopping/

  Gentoo     compile everything from source, tuned to your CPU
             $ emerge firefox
             -> see you next week

  NixOS      your whole OS in one config file: reproducible, roll back
             anything. "works on my machine" -> now reproducibly on
             yours

  Void       independent & rolling; runit instead of systemd;
             glibc or musl, your pick

  Alpine     tiny: musl + BusyBox, security-minded.
             your Docker image's favourite distro

  Slackware  since 1993: the oldest distro still maintained.
             older than most people in this room
