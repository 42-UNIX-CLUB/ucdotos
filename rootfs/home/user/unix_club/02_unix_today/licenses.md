# Licenses: permissive vs copyleft

  $ diff -y bsd.license gpl.license

  permissive . BSD, MIT                copyleft . GPL
  ✓ use it, change it, sell it,        ✓ use it, change it, sell it
    close it
  ! just keep the copyright notice     ! distribute it -> share the
                                         source too, under the same
                                         license

  -> why Apple (macOS) & Sony          -> improvements stay open for
     (PlayStation) could build            everyone

# tl;dr

  BSD:          do whatever, keep our name.
  GPL:          do whatever, share it back.
  proprietary:  do nothing, pay us.
