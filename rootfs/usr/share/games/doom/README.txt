Doom looks for its game data (an IWAD file) in this directory, then in the
current directory. Put one of these here:

  freedoom1.wad  Freedoom Phase 1, free content under a BSD licence,
                 from https://github.com/freedoom/freedoom/releases
  doom1.wad      the Doom shareware episode (version 1.9), freely
                 distributable unmodified under id Software's shareware terms

or start doom with -iwad /path/to/file.wad.
